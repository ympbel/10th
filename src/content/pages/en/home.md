Started in 2016 at the Department of Radiation Oncology, Yonsei University College of Medicine, the lab's work has followed four strands: CT reconstruction, medical AI, radiation therapy and particle therapy. This site records the people who passed through the lab over those ten years and the papers, patents, presentations, awards and projects they left behind.
